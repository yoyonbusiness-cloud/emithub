Patch Notes
Latest updates and features in EmitHub.
August 2026 Release August 2026
  • Direct-to-Disk Saving: Normally, browsers dump everything you download into your RAM until the transfer is finished. If you try downloading a 5GB–50GB file, your browser runs out of memory and crashes. Direct-to-Disk fixes this by writing incoming chunks straight to your hard drive in real time. Your browser uses basically zero RAM, meaning you can send massive files with no tab crashes.
  • Local Nearby Drop: Discover and send files directly to devices on the same Wi-Fi network without typing codes or copying links.
  • Feedback & Reports: Send bug reports or ideas directly to the admin from right inside the app.
  • Security Shield: Safety warnings and confirmations before saving executable or suspicious file types.
