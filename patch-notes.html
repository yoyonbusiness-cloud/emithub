Direct-to-Disk Saving
Performance

Normally, browsers dump everything you download into your RAM until the transfer finishes. If you try downloading a 5GB–50GB file, your browser runs out of memory and crashes. Direct-to-Disk fixes this by writing incoming chunks straight to your hard drive in real time. Your browser uses basically zero RAM, so you can transfer huge files without any tab crashes.
